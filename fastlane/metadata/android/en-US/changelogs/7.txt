0.5.1
- The camera no longer dies after a night shot: opening the gallery, switching camera or changing mode now waits until the photo is saved (closing the camera during the long exposures left the phone with no camera until a reboot)
- The interface stays smooth while a multi-frame photo is processed, and the shutter shows how far the photo is
- Burst frames are matched with their own exposure, so a frame taken with the wrong settings is left out instead of being merged too bright or too dark
- Autofocus is locked for the whole burst
